<span style="color:#9e9e9e">在一种类似“石头、剪刀、布”的求偶策略中，大个头橙喉蜥蜴可以赶走中等体型的蓝喉蜥蜴，而蓝喉蜥蜴<span style="color:red"><strong>反过来</strong></span>又能<span style="color:red"><strong>密切关注</strong></span>自己的单一配偶。</span>

<span style="color:#9e9e9e">这些蜥蜴<span style="color:red"><strong>面临……与……之间的权衡</strong></span>：数量充足与确认成本。</span>

<span style="color:#9e9e9e">这种权衡也越来越<span style="color:red"><strong>为……所关注</strong></span>人类。</span>

<span style="color:#9e9e9e">生产文字、视频或音频的成本已经下降；如今这样做只意味着在聊天机器人上输入几个词，而不再是数小时的努力。</span>

<span style="color:red"><strong>As a consequence</strong></span>, roughly half of articles published on the internet last year were AI-generated.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e"><span style="color:red"><strong>因此</strong></span>，去年互联网上发布的文章大约有一半是由人工智能生成的。</span>

### Paragraph 3

How can internet users <span style="color:red"><strong>distinguish</strong></span> an article by a genuine but little-known news website <span style="color:red"><strong>from</strong></span> outright misinformation?
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">互联网用户如何<span style="color:red"><strong>把……与……区分开来</strong></span>：一篇由真实但知名度较低的新闻网站发布的文章，以及彻头彻尾的虚假信息？</span>

<span style="color:#9e9e9e">市场参与者通常<span style="color:red"><strong>依靠</strong></span>“昂贵信号”<span style="color:red"><strong>来做出</strong></span>这样的选择。</span>

The certificate <span style="color:red"><strong>allows</strong></span> employers <span style="color:red"><strong>to distinguish between</strong></span> good and bad applicants—creating what economists <span style="color:red"><strong>call</strong></span> a “separating equilibrium” and enabling transactions between willing participants.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">证书<span style="color:red"><strong>使</strong></span>雇主<span style="color:red"><strong>能够区分</strong></span>优秀与不合格的申请者——从而形成经济学家<span style="color:red"><strong>称为</strong></span>“分离均衡”的状态，并促成自愿参与者之间的交易。</span>

### Paragraph 4

During an earlier information age, newspaper branding provided a costly signal.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">在较早的信息时代，报纸品牌提供了一种昂贵信号。</span>

In what was, in game-theoretic terms, a “reputation game” newspapers would, issue by issue, reveal exactly how focused on the truth they were.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">用博弈论术语来说，在一种“声誉博弈”中，报纸会一期接一期地准确显示它们对事实真相有多么重视。</span>

Repeated interactions with a reader, when they were deciding which paper <span style="color:red"><strong>to purchase</strong></span>, constituted a separating equilibrium.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">当读者决定<span style="color:red"><strong>购买哪份</strong></span>报纸时，报纸与读者之间反复的互动构成了分离均衡。</span>

Content farms, websites that copied reputable news pages but with made-up stories, however, can <span style="color:red"><strong>sneak into</strong></span> these brands like the yellow lizards.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">然而，内容农场，即那些复制知名新闻网页却配上编造故事的网站，能够像黄喉蜥蜴一样<span style="color:red"><strong>偷偷混入</strong></span>这些品牌之中。</span>

### Paragraph 5

Thus the “separating equilibrium” is devolving into a “pooling equilibrium”, in which there is <span style="color:red"><strong>no way of</strong></span> telling between one type of creation and another.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">因此，“分离均衡”正在退化为“混同均衡”，在这种状态下，人们<span style="color:red"><strong>没有办法</strong></span>区分一种作品与另一种作品。</span>

The problem with a “pooling equilibrium”, as George Akerlof <span style="color:red"><strong>put it</strong></span> in a Nobel-prizewinning paper, is that “Dishonest dealings tend to drive honest dealings out of the market.”
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">正如乔治·阿克洛夫在一篇获得诺贝尔奖的论文中<span style="color:red"><strong>所说</strong></span>，“混同均衡”的问题在于：“不诚实交易往往会把诚实交易挤出市场。”</span>

Without a way to <span style="color:red"><strong>tell</strong></span> good <span style="color:red"><strong>from</strong></span> bad, buyers treat everything as bad.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">如果没有办法<span style="color:red"><strong>把</strong></span>好<span style="color:red"><strong>与</strong></span>坏<span style="color:red"><strong>区分开来</strong></span>，买家就会把一切都视为坏的。</span>

### Paragraph 6

Among side-blotched lizards, the rock-paper-scissors nature of the lizards’ mating strategies means that if any of the colours becomes too common, one of the other types receives an advantage.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">在侧斑蜥蜴中，蜥蜴求偶策略的“石头、剪刀、布”性质意味着，如果任何一种颜色变得过于常见，其他某一种类型就会获得优势。</span>

Optimistically, then, an outpouring in the productivity of misinformation generators may <span style="color:red"><strong>provide</strong></span> a similar advantage <span style="color:red"><strong>to</strong></span> the journalistic equivalent of the blue-throated lizards—publications that jealously <span style="color:red"><strong>guard their reputation for</strong></span> truth-telling.
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">那么，乐观地说，虚假信息生成者生产力的大爆发，可能会<span style="color:red"><strong>给……提供</strong></span>类似优势；这一优势属于新闻界中相当于蓝喉蜥蜴的群体——那些谨慎<span style="color:red"><strong>维护其……声誉</strong></span>的、以讲真话著称的出版物。</span>

## 三、题目原文、定位与详细解析

### 31. Why does the author mention the example of side-blotched lizards in Paragraph 1?
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">31. 作者为什么在第一段提到侧斑蜥蜴的例子？</span>

> <span style="color:#9e9e9e">原文定位：Paragraph 1—2。Paragraph 1 描写三种蜥蜴的策略，尤其是黄喉蜥蜴“sneak into the big males’ group of mates”；Paragraph 2 紧接着指出“The lizards face a trade-off between abundance and the cost of confirmation. It is a trade-off that is increasingly of concern to humans, too.” 即蜥蜴例子是为了引出人类社会中“确认成本”下降后真假难辨的问题。</span>

| 选项 | 英文与中文 | 判断 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | To share a popular trend. <br><span style="color:#9e9e9e">为了分享一种流行趋势。</span> | 错误 | 原文并没有说侧斑蜥蜴的求偶策略是“popular trend”。第一段是生物例子，第二段立刻转向“trade-off”和“humans”，说明其作用不是分享趋势，而是引入后文问题。 |
| B | To reveal a disturbing issue. <br><span style="color:#9e9e9e">为了揭示一个令人担忧的问题。</span> | 正确 | 第一段写黄喉蜥蜴能“sneak into the big males’ group of mates”，第二段说这种“trade-off”也越来越“of concern to humans”。随后作者指出 AI 生成内容成本下降，导致真假信息更难确认。因此蜥蜴例子用于揭示“确认成本下降、虚假信息混入真实信息”的令人担忧问题。 |
| C | To criticize a costly trade-off. <br><span style="color:#9e9e9e">为了批评一种代价高昂的权衡。</span> | 错误 | 原文确实出现“The lizards face a trade-off between abundance and the cost of confirmation”，但作者不是批评该权衡本身，而是借该权衡引出人类互联网信息环境中的问题。C 项把写作目的误解为“批评 trade-off”。 |
| D | To introduce a mating strategy. <br><span style="color:#9e9e9e">为了介绍一种求偶策略。</span> | 错误 | 第一段表面上确实介绍了 mating strategies，但阅读理解的写作意图不能只看表层内容。第二段“It is a trade-off that is increasingly of concern to humans, too”说明作者真正目的在于类比人类信息社会中的问题，而不是单纯科普求偶策略。 |

**本题答案：B。**

### 32. According to the passage, which of the following is an example of separating equilibrium?
<span style="color:#9e9e9e">32. 根据文章，下列哪一项是“分离均衡”的例子？</span>

> <span style="color:#9e9e9e">原文定位：Paragraph 3。原文说：“An employer looking for a new hire ... may seek applicants with a degree that took effort and a certain amount of skill ... The certificate allows employers to distinguish between good and bad applicants—creating what economists call a ‘separating equilibrium’...” 即“需要投入努力和技能的学位证书”能够帮助雇主区分申请者，这就是分离均衡。</span>

| 选项 | 英文与中文 | 判断 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Firms sustain high-input advertising campaigns to reflect quality. <br><span style="color:#9e9e9e">公司持续开展高投入广告活动来体现质量。</span> | 正确 | 该项中的“high-input advertising campaigns”与原文“a degree that took effort and a certain amount of skill”逻辑一致：二者都是成本较高、较难伪造的“costly signals”，可以帮助市场参与者区分质量高低，因此属于 separating equilibrium。 |
| B | We tend to learn a common language regardless of native tongue. <br><span style="color:#9e9e9e">无论母语是什么，我们往往会学习一种共同语言。</span> | 错误 | 原文强调的是通过“costly signals”区分不同质量或类型，例如学历证书区分“good and bad applicants”。学习共同语言是趋同，不是区分类型，因此更接近“pooling”而非“separating”。 |
| C | The expense of acquiring education is similar across ability types. <br><span style="color:#9e9e9e">不同能力类型的人获得教育的花费相似。</span> | 错误 | 原文中证书之所以有效，是因为它“took effort and a certain amount of skill”，能显示申请者的能力。如果不同能力者获取教育成本相似，信号就无法有效区分好坏申请者，不能构成分离均衡。 |
| D | Socially-responsible and profit-driven firms spend equally on charity. <br><span style="color:#9e9e9e">具有社会责任感的公司和逐利型公司在慈善上的花费相同。</span> | 错误 | 该项中两类公司“spend equally”，无法让外界区分其真实动机。原文说 separating equilibrium 的核心是“distinguish between good and bad applicants”。如果投入相同，就不能区分类型。 |

**本题答案：A。**

## 五、篇章主旨归纳

本文先用侧斑蜥蜴三种求偶策略作类比，说明当“模仿者”可以低成本混入真实群体时，识别成本会变得关键。随后，作者把这一逻辑迁移到互联网信息环境中：AI 使文字、视频和音频的生产成本大幅下降，虚假内容更容易模仿真实新闻。过去，报纸品牌和长期声誉是读者判断真实性的“昂贵信号”，能够形成“分离均衡”；但当内容农场和 AI 生成内容大量出现时，真实与虚假之间的边界被削弱，市场可能走向“混同均衡”。不过，作者最后也提出乐观可能：虚假信息越泛滥，越可能凸显那些坚持真实报道、珍视声誉的出版物的价值。
