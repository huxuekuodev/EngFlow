# 2026年丰台区高三二模英语阅读解析（C、D篇）

## 阅读 C 篇

### 【原文逐句对照与考点解析】

Whales are some of the most highly intelligent species on Earth. 
<font color="lightgrey">鲸鱼是地球上最聪明的物种之一。</font>

Each species of whale has a distinct communication system that often varies within populations. 
<font color="lightgrey">每种鲸鱼都有独特的通信系统，且在种群内部通常有所不同。</font>

Since humans have been researching and observing these forms of communication, a question has surfaced: Could humans one day **<font color="red">engage in</font>** acoustic interactions with whales? 
<font color="lightgrey">自从人类开始研究和观察这些交流形式以来，一个问题浮出了水面：人类有一天能**参与**与鲸鱼的声音互动吗？</font>

And what could these interactions reveal about non-human intelligence? 
<font color="lightgrey">这些互动能揭示关于非人类智能的什么信息？</font>

Researchers from the University of California and the Alaska Whale Foundation have been exploring whale-to-human communication with humpback whales, and they’ve **<font color="red">come up with</font>** some fascinating conclusions. 
<font color="lightgrey">加州大学和阿拉斯加鲸鱼基金会的研究人员一直在探索座头鲸与人类之间的交流，并**提出**了一些迷人的结论。</font>

When scientists recorded a humpback whale contact call, known as a whup, and played it underwater around their research vessel, they were surprised when a whale named Twain became a more than willing participant in the **<font color="red">call-and-response</font>** exchange. 
<font color="lightgrey">当科学家们录制了一种被称为“whup”的座头鲸联络呼叫，并在他们的研究船周围的水下播放时，他们惊讶地发现一只名叫Twain的鲸鱼非常愿意参与这种**呼叫与回应**的交流。</font>

Scientists produced a single sound, or whup call, that Twain would then respond with her own whup call to them, a “conversation” described in their PeerJ paper. 
<font color="lightgrey">科学家发出一个声音，即whup呼叫，Twain随后会用她自己的whup呼叫回应他们，这在他们的PeerJ论文中被描述为一场“对话”。</font>

“It might loosely translate to ‘hello’ or some sort of greeting call for when animals **<font color="red">come together</font>** or just to signal that individuals are in a certain location,” says Dawson Hubbard, study author and animal behaviourist with Whale SETI. 
<font color="lightgrey">“它可能大致翻译为‘你好’，或者某种当动物们**聚集在一起**时的问候呼叫，或者只是为了发出个体在某个特定位置的信号，”研究作者、Whale SETI的动物行为学家Dawson Hubbard说。</font>

In this case, it was as if Twain was either saying “hello” to scientists or “I’m over here.” 
<font color="lightgrey">在这种情况下，就好像Twain要么是在向科学家们说“你好”，要么是在说“我在这儿”。</font>

But in both instances, she was responding to and **<font color="red">engaging with</font>** whale signals that humans produced. 
<font color="lightgrey">但在两种情况下，她都在回应并**参与**人类发出的鲸鱼信号。</font>

Of course, whether whales want to communicate with humans varies by the temperament of the whale and by the situation. 
<font color="lightgrey">当然，鲸鱼是否想与人类交流取决于鲸鱼的性格和具体情况。</font>

Just like some humans are more extroverted than others, this could also **<font color="red">be true for</font>** whales, although this would need more research to understand. 
<font color="lightgrey">就像有些人类比其他人更外向一样，这对鲸鱼来说可能也**适用**，尽管这需要更多的研究来理解。</font>

Twain circled around the scientists’ boat and was **<font color="red">in proximity to</font>** its speakers. 
<font color="lightgrey">Twain在科学家的船周围盘旋，并**靠近**其扬声器。</font>

Permit limitations only allow for researchers to produce the sound for 20 minutes, but once they stopped, Twain continued to produce more signals before leaving. 
<font color="lightgrey">许可限制只允许研究人员发出20分钟的声音，但一旦他们停止，Twain在离开前继续发出了更多的信号。</font>

“What’s cool about Twain is that she could have left **<font color="red">at any point</font>**, as it wasn’t a caged environment where she couldn’t leave, so she was choosing to be close to us,” says Hubbard. 
<font color="lightgrey">“Twain的酷之处在于她**在任何时候**都可以离开，因为这不是一个她无法离开的笼养环境，所以她是选择靠近我们的，”Hubbard说。</font>

Often, a willingness to communicate with humans would be **<font color="red">food-mediated</font>**, but in this case, it wasn’t. 
<font color="lightgrey">通常，与人类交流的意愿是由**食物介导**的，但在这种情况下，事实并非如此。</font>

There have also been other examples. 
<font color="lightgrey">还有其他的例子。</font>

Gray whales in the San Ignacio lagoon in Mexico, for example, are famously friendly, in fact, some gray whales regularly **<font color="red">approach</font>** boats and allow visitors to **<font color="red">have gentle contact with</font>** them. 
<font color="lightgrey">例如，墨西哥圣伊格纳西奥泻湖的灰鲸以友好著称，事实上，一些灰鲸经常**靠近**船只，并允许游客与它们**进行温柔的接触**。</font>

According to the Oceanic Society, this behaviour isn’t shared amongst all gray whales, and in fact, this species of whale was almost hunted to extinction just a generation ago. 
<font color="lightgrey">根据海洋协会的说法，这种行为并非所有灰鲸共有，事实上，这个物种的鲸鱼就在一代人之前几乎被猎杀到灭绝。</font>

But the behaviour may have been **<font color="red">passed down through</font>** generations, and some gray whales in this population voluntarily approach humans, in the end, these and other encounters show that, in some cases, individual whales appear willing to **<font color="red">engage with</font>** humans. 
<font color="lightgrey">但这种行为可能已经**代代相传**，这个种群中的一些灰鲸自愿靠近人类，最后，这些和其他遭遇表明，在某些情况下，个体鲸鱼似乎愿意与人类**互动**。</font>

And the more we learn about their mode of communication and what they are saying to us, the more we can learn about them. 
<font color="lightgrey">我们对它们的交流模式以及它们对我们说的话了解得越多，我们就能对它们了解得越多。</font>

### 【题目详细解析】

**28. To study whales, the scientists ________.**
<font color="lightgrey">为了研究鲸鱼，科学家们做了什么？</font>
<font color="lightgrey">原文定位：When scientists recorded a humpback whale contact call... and played it underwater around their research vessel... (Para. 2)</font>

*   **A. caught whales for controlled experiments**
    *   **错误原因**：与原文相悖。原文提到“it wasn’t a caged environment”（这不是一个笼养环境），说明没有捕捉鲸鱼。
*   **B. trained whales to converse with humans**
    *   **错误原因**：无中生有。文中提到的是鲸鱼自愿的回应，而非经过人工训练。
*   **C. responded to whales with recorded calls**
    *   **错误原因**：逻辑颠倒。是科学家先播放录音（played it underwater），鲸鱼随后回应（Twain would then respond）。
*   **D. played whale calls to interact with them**
    *   **正确依据**：原文明确提到科学家录制了whup呼叫并在水下播放（recorded a humpback whale contact call... and played it underwater），目的是观察鲸鱼的反应并进行互动。

**29. What can we infer from the passage?**
<font color="lightgrey">从文章中可以推断出什么？</font>
<font color="lightgrey">原文定位：But the behaviour may have been passed down through generations... (Para. 6)</font>

*   **A. The maximum duration of whales’ response is 20 minutes.**
    *   **错误原因**：偷换概念。20分钟是“Permit limitations”（许可限制）规定的研究人员播放声音的时间，而非鲸鱼回应的极限。
*   **B. Gray whales’ friendly behaviour may be a learned feature.**
    *   **正确依据**：原文提到灰鲸的友好行为“may have been passed down through generations”（可能已经代代相传），这暗示了这是一种习得的特征（learned feature）。
*   **C. Whales’ personality decides their willingness to interact.**
    *   **错误原因**：表述过于绝对。原文说“varies by the temperament... and by the situation”（取决于性格……和具体情况），且提到“would need more research to understand”，目前只是推测。
*   **D. Humpback whales respond to humans for food rewards.**
    *   **错误原因**：与原文相悖。原文明确指出“in this case, it wasn’t [food-mediated]”（在这种情况下，不是食物介导的）。

**30. Which of the following can be the best title for the passage?**
<font color="lightgrey">以下哪项是文章的最佳标题？</font>
<font color="lightgrey">核心思想：文章通过座头鲸Twain和灰鲸的例子，探讨了鲸鱼是否愿意以及如何与人类进行交流互动。</font>

*   **A. Decoding the Talk Between Humans and Whales**
    *   **错误原因**：范围过窄。文章不仅讨论了“解码”，更讨论了鲸鱼互动的意愿。
*   **B. Are Whales Willing to Talk with Humans?**
    *   **正确依据**：文章从Twain的自愿互动出发，讨论了性格、环境、非食物诱导等因素，最后总结“individual whales appear willing to engage with humans”。这个标题完美契合了文章探讨的核心问题。
*   **C. Could Humans Ever Talk with Whales?**
    *   **错误原因**：时态不符。文章已经展示了人类正在与鲸鱼进行初步的“对话”，而非仅仅探讨未来的可能性。
*   **D. Unlocking the Secret of Whale Whups**
    *   **错误原因**：以偏概全。“Whup”只是文中提到的一个具体呼叫类型，不能代表全篇关于鲸鱼互动意愿的讨论。

## 阅读 D 篇

### 【原文逐句对照与考点解析】

What time is it? It’s such a basic question and provokes me to **<font color="red">take a look at</font>** time in the context of both humans and artificial intelligence. 
<font color="lightgrey">现在几点了？这是一个如此基本的问题，它促使我**审视**人类和人工智能背景下的时间。</font>

Simply put, AI operates inside the now, the perpetual present. 
<font color="lightgrey">简单来说，AI在“当下”，即永恒的现在运行。</font>

Yet humans construct meaning across time. 
<font color="lightgrey">然而，人类跨越时间构建意义。</font>

This “temporal divide” creates a key distinction, if not a conflict, that’s **<font color="red">worthy of</font>** a deeper look. 
<font color="lightgrey">这种“时间鸿沟”创造了一个关键的区别，如果不是冲突的话，这**值得**深入研究。</font>

Typically, we evaluate artificial intelligence by capability, which includes things like speed, accuracy, fluency, and even scale. 
<font color="lightgrey">通常，我们根据能力来评估人工智能，这包括速度、准确性、流畅度甚至规模等因素。</font>

But my take is that this perspective misses something that is both critical and deeply human. 
<font color="lightgrey">但我的看法是，这种视角忽略了一些既关键又极具人性化的东西。</font>

Humans build meaning through continuity, and this includes memory, revision, anticipation, and the lived accumulation of experience. 
<font color="lightgrey">人类通过连续性构建意义，这包括记忆、修正、预期以及生活经验的积累。</font>

AI does not. 
<font color="lightgrey">AI则不然。</font>

For us, meaning is shaped across duration. 
<font color="lightgrey">对我们来说，意义是在一段时间内形成的。</font>

We don’t form understanding from isolated frames. 
<font color="lightgrey">我们不会从孤立的画面中形成理解。</font>

We learn from many moments that inform and reshape each other. 
<font color="lightgrey">我们从许多相互告知和重塑的时刻中学习。</font>

The reliability of our beliefs **<font color="red">depends on</font>** that slow integration. 
<font color="lightgrey">我们信念的可靠性**取决于**那种缓慢的整合。</font>

It’s how understanding matures into something stable, or perhaps better said, human. 
<font color="lightgrey">这就是理解如何成熟为某种稳定的东西，或者或许更好说，人性的东西。</font>

AI collapses time into immediacy. 
<font color="lightgrey">AI将时间压缩为即时性。</font>

Each output **<font color="red">stands alone</font>** without reference to what came before and without responsibility toward what may follow. 
<font color="lightgrey">每个输出都是**独立存在**的，不参考之前的内容，也不对之后可能发生的内容负责。</font>

This is why AI can be so persuasive, as it produces coherence without the weight of history behind it. 
<font color="lightgrey">这就是为什么AI如此具有说服力，因为它在没有历史负担的情况下产生了连贯性。</font>

A recent study that examined reasoning tasks illustrates this rather clearly. 
<font color="lightgrey">最近一项检查推理任务的研究相当清楚地说明了这一点。</font>

AI assistance led participants to perform slightly better, but to feel dramatically more improved than they actually were. 
<font color="lightgrey">AI辅助使参与者表现得稍微好一点，但让他们感觉比实际进步大得多。</font>

This illusion emerges because instant coherence (tech) feels like internal mastery (human). 
<font color="lightgrey">这种错觉之所以出现，是因为即时的连贯性（技术）感觉像是内在的精通（人类）。</font>

Anyone who has used AI to summarise a concept has felt this. 
<font color="lightgrey">任何使用过AI来总结概念的人都有过这种感觉。</font>

It happens when you read a smooth, confident explanation and suddenly believe you now “understand” it without any of the internal struggle that produces true understanding. 
<font color="lightgrey">当你读到一个流畅、自信的解释，并突然相信你现在“理解”了它，而没有任何产生真正理解所需的内在挣扎时，这种情况就会发生。</font>

The mind confuses the appearance of cognition with the acquisition of cognition. 
<font color="lightgrey">大脑将认知的表象与认知的获得混为一谈。</font>

And the key distinction is that AI shortens the distance between exposure and confidence, not between exposure and wisdom. 
<font color="lightgrey">关键的区别在于，AI缩短了接触和自信之间的距离，而不是接触和智慧之间的距离。</font>

I think this matters because humans may begin adapting to the temporal logic of machines. 
<font color="lightgrey">我认为这很重要，因为人类可能开始适应机器的时间逻辑。</font>

If present-tense coherence becomes more rewarding than the slower accumulation of meaning, we could begin to trade our temporal cognition for the immediacy AI offers. 
<font color="lightgrey">如果现在时态的连贯性比缓慢的意义积累更有回报，我们可能会开始用我们的时间认知来换取AI提供的即时性。</font>

The risk is not replacement, but more of a dissociation from the very structure of meaning-making that defines the human mind. 
<font color="lightgrey">风险不在于被取代，而更多在于与定义人类思维的意义构建结构的脱节。</font>

The real question, as I have relentlessly asked, is not whether AI will think like we do. 
<font color="lightgrey">正如我坚持不懈地问的那样，真正的问题不是AI是否会像我们一样思考。</font>

The question is whether we will continue to think like ourselves. 
<font color="lightgrey">问题是我们是否会继续像我们自己一样思考。</font>

To defend the narrative arc is far from a poetic gesture. 
<font color="lightgrey">捍卫叙事弧线绝非一种诗意的姿态。</font>

It’s an essential cognitive practice. 
<font color="lightgrey">这是一种基本的认知实践。</font>

For me, it means tolerating slower understanding when speed is seductive. 
<font color="lightgrey">对我来说，这意味着当速度具有诱惑力时，要容忍较慢的理解。</font>

It means returning to the longer thread of experience when instant fluency tempts us. 
<font color="lightgrey">这意味着当即时的流畅诱惑我们时，要回到更长的经验线索中。</font>

It means remembering that wisdom requires the friction of time. 
<font color="lightgrey">这意味着要记住，智慧需要时间的摩擦。</font>

And that’s something we all should consider. 
<font color="lightgrey">这是我们所有人都应该考虑的事情。</font>

Meaning is temporal. Story is temporal. Identity is temporal. AI does not live there. We do. 
<font color="lightgrey">意义是时间的。故事是时间的。身份是时间的。AI不生活在那里。我们生活在那里。</font>

### 【题目详细解析】

**31. What does the “temporal divide” in paragraph 1 refer to?**
<font color="lightgrey">第1段中的“temporal divide”指的是什么？</font>
<font color="lightgrey">原文定位：Simply put, AI operates inside the now... Yet humans construct meaning across time. (Para. 1)</font>

*   **A. A distinction in evaluating AI’s competencies.**
    *   **错误原因**：张冠李戴。评估能力（评估AI的速度、准确性等）是第2段提到的内容，而非“时间鸿沟”的定义。
*   **B. A gap between present living and past reflection.**
    *   **错误原因**：范围过窄。这只是人类构建意义的一部分，没有体现出AI与人类的区别。
*   **C. A difference in how humans and AI make meaning.**
    *   **正确依据**：原文明确指出AI在“now”（当下）运行，而人类“construct meaning across time”（跨越时间构建意义）。这种在构建意义方式上的时间差异正是“temporal divide”。
*   **D. A divide in time management between humans and AI.**
    *   **错误原因**：词义误解。这里讨论的是认知和意义构建的逻辑，而非日常的“时间管理”。

**32. As for the result of the study examining reasoning skills, the author feels ________.**
<font color="lightgrey">对于检查推理能力的研究结果，作者感到怎样？</font>
<font color="lightgrey">原文定位：The risk is not replacement, but more of a dissociation from the very structure of meaning-making... The question is whether we will continue to think like ourselves. (Para. 6-7)</font>

*   **A. worried**
    *   **正确依据**：作者提到AI带来的“illusion”（错觉）让人们误以为自己精通了，并警告说人类可能会“trade our temporal cognition for the immediacy AI offers”，面临与意义构建结构脱节的风险。最后呼吁要“defend the narrative arc”，体现了对人类思维方式可能被改变的担忧。
*   **B. surprised**
    *   **错误原因**：无中生有。作者对研究结果进行了深入分析，但并未表现出惊讶。
*   **C. doubtful**
    *   **错误原因**：曲解原意。作者并不怀疑研究结果，而是对研究揭示的现象（错觉）感到担忧。
*   **D. sympathetic**
    *   **错误原因**：词义不符。作者是在进行理性的批判和警示，而非表达同情。

**33. How does the author mainly develop the passage?**
<font color="lightgrey">作者主要如何展开文章？</font>
<font color="lightgrey">核心逻辑：文章开头引入AI与人类在时间逻辑上的区别（concept），随后通过对比（contrast）两者构建意义的方式，并引用研究来进一步阐述这种对比带来的影响。</font>

*   **A. By raising questions and solving them one by one.**
    *   **错误原因**：不符合事实。作者提出了问题，但更多是在引发思考和警示，而非提供具体的“解决方案”。
*   **B. By citing studies and evaluating their limitations.**
    *   **错误原因**：以偏概全。引用研究只是为了说明“错觉”这一论点，而非文章的主要结构。
*   **C. By presenting a study and drawing a conclusion.**
    *   **错误原因**：结构描述不准。文章是先提出核心观点，再用研究辅助说明，最后升华。
*   **D. By introducing a concept and making contrasts.**
    *   **正确依据**：文章首先引入了“temporal divide”这一概念，然后通篇在对比人类（跨越时间、缓慢整合、智慧）与AI（即时、孤立、流畅表象）的不同。

**34. What can we learn from the passage?**
<font color="lightgrey">从文章中我们可以学到什么？</font>
<font color="lightgrey">原文定位：It means remembering that wisdom requires the friction of time. (Para. 7)</font>

*   **A. Exposure + Confidence = Mastery**
    *   **错误原因**：这是文中提到的“illusion”（错觉），作者认为这并不是真正的精通。
*   **B. Coherence + Immediacy = Logic**
    *   **错误原因**：这是AI的特征，作者认为这种即时的连贯性会让人脱节。
*   **C. Duration + Friction = Wisdom**
    *   **正确依据**：原文最后一段提到“wisdom requires the friction of time”（智慧需要时间的摩擦），并且强调意义是在“duration”（时长）中形成的。这完美对应了选项。
*   **D. Speed + Fluency = Insight**
    *   **错误原因**：与原文相悖。作者认为速度和流畅度是诱惑，会阻碍真正的理解和洞察。
